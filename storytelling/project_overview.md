# Insurance Risk & Claims Analytics Project

## 1. Project Context

This project simulates a real-world analytics environment at Ubuntu Insurance Ltd.

The company operates in the insurance sector and is currently experiencing:

- Increasing claim costs
- Suspected fraudulent claim activity
- Uneven customer risk distribution
- Pressure on profitability

As a Junior Data Analyst, the goal of this project is to analyze insurance data and generate insights that support better business decision-making.

---

## 2. Business Objective

The purpose of this analysis is to:

- Understand customer and policy behavior
- Identify high-risk policy types
- Detect potential fraud patterns
- Analyze claim trends over time
- Support data-driven pricing and risk strategies

---

## 3. Dataset Description

The dataset contains a single flat table:

**dfa_insurance_project_raw_dataset**

It includes information about:

- Customers (age, gender, income, location)
- Policies (type, status, premium amount)
- Claims (claim amount, status, date)
- Fraud indicators

---

## 4. Analytics Approach

The project was completed in four stages:

### 1. Data Exploration (SQL)
- Profiled dataset structure
- Calculated totals (customers, policies, claims)
- Identified trends in claims and fraud
- Performed grouping and aggregation analysis

### 2. Data Cleaning (SQL)
- Removed duplicate records
- Standardized inconsistent text values
- Handled missing and null values
- Fixed incorrect data types
- Removed invalid records (e.g. negative claims)
- Standardized fraud indicators

### 3. Data Visualization (Power BI)
Built interactive dashboards to visualize:

- Claims performance
- Fraud distribution
- Customer segmentation
- Policy risk analysis

### 4. Business Storytelling
Converted analysis into business insights and recommendations.

---

## 5. Key Business Questions Answered

This analysis focused on answering:

- What trends exist in insurance claims?
- Which policy types carry the highest risk?
- Where is fraud most common?
- Which customer groups generate the most risk?

---

## 6. Expected Business Outcomes

The insights from this project support:

- Improved fraud detection strategies
- Better pricing and premium adjustments
- Identification of high-risk customers
- Enhanced profitability through risk management
